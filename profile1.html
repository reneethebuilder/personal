[ Product Approach ]

Technology is useful when it makes something meaningfully better.

01

Understand before building.

Start with the customer problem, context and opportunity. Technology is not the strategy by itself.

02

Bring structure to ambiguity.

Perfect requirements rarely arrive first. I frame the problem, surface assumptions, define options and create a path toward evidence.

03

Make the tradeoffs visible.

Good product strategy is as much about what not to build as what to build — connecting customer value, feasibility and business impact.

04

Measure what changed.

Shipping is not the outcome. Adoption, engagement, retention, efficiency, revenue, risk and customer value tell us whether the product actually worked.
